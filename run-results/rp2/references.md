# RP2 Background References

This file lists the background sources used to frame the RP2 packing case study. PDFs and extracted paper text are not included in the public run-results folder.

## Shevchishin--Smirnov

Role in the cluster:

- Provides the sharp triangle-inequality criterion for Lagrangian `RP2` in the threefold blowup case.
- Serves as the finite low-blowup model that the many-blowup question generalizes.

## Evans

Role in the cluster:

- Frames nonorientable Lagrangian nonsqueezing and smooth isotopy questions.
- Emphasizes why nonorientable Lagrangians behave differently under deformation of the ambient symplectic form.

## Adaloglou

Role in the cluster:

- Connects Lagrangian projective planes, pinwheels, rational blowups, disjunction, and homology classes.
- Helps organize the transition from finite low-blowup classification to many-blowup behavior.
